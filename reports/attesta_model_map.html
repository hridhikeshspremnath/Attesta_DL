How it trains

z    = (log1p(x) − μ) / σ       # train-set stats
p    = 1 / (1 + e^−logit)
risk = round(1000 · p)
L    = BCEpos_w(main) + 0.3 · Σk=1..5 BCE(auxk)
H    = −Σ pi log2 pi          # text entropy feature

AdamW, lr 1e-3, effective batch 32 (4 × 8 accumulation), fp16 on a 4 GB laptop GPU, early stop on validation PR-AUC. About 2 minutes per epoch, stops after 6 to 11 epochs. The five auxiliary heads are install-time exec, network, credential access, obfuscation and process spawn.

Questions a sceptic will ask

  • 0.915Can file paths alone give it away?A classifier on path headers only reaches this AUC. Part of the model's score may reflect how the data was collected, not behaviour.
  • 0.849Does the upload date leak?Malicious dates are discovery dates, benign are publish dates. Year plus ecosystem alone separates the classes at this AUC.
  • 0.44What does a flag mean in real traffic?At 1% malware prevalence and 1% false positives, about 44% of flagged packages would be truly malicious. The scores are not calibrated yet.
Test split, 4,275 packages. Results are from the first full run and two more seeds; recall at 0.1% false positives is noisy because only about 2,600 benign packages set that threshold.
